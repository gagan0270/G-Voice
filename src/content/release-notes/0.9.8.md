# Welcome to G-Voice v0.9.8

**G-Voice** is an ultra-fast, 100% private, offline AI voice typing assistant created by **Gagan V**.

## Key Highlights

- **Instant Speech-to-Text**: Press `Alt + C` or `Ctrl + Space` anywhere in any app to speak and have your words transcribed instantly.
- **Real-Time Streaming**: Powered by Parakeet Unified FastConformer-TDT, transcribing text in real time as you speak with sub-150ms latency.
- **Multilingual Whisper Support**: Seamlessly transcribe English, Kannada (`kn`), and over 90+ languages offline with Whisper Base Q8_0.
- **Zero Cloud / 100% Private**: Your microphone audio never leaves your device. All neural inference runs locally on your CPU.
- **Zero Cold-Start Lag**: Models stay resident in RAM for instant, effortless dictation whenever you speak.
- **Lightweight & Efficient**: Uses minimal system resources (~45 MB RAM idle).

Developed with precision by **Gagan V**.
